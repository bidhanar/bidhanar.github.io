On Rotors and Rain

26 Feb 2025

The rotor hum remembers the rain,
a steady whisper on the canopy of sky.
I steer between two silent maps—
one of machines, the other of the heart.

Maps that fold and breathe, and when opened,
reveal a small, bright island—routine and wonder.
We land where the wind allows, and write our names
in the mud of mornings that taste like solder and tea.
