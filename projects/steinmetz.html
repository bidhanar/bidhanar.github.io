Analyzing Steinmetz Dataset

Summer Project — Brain & Cognitive Society (May 2021 – Jul 2021)

Overview

Exploratory and predictive analysis on the Steinmetz neural dataset to discover links between behaviour and neuron firing rates.

What I did

  • Visualized neural responses using Matplotlib and Seaborn to discover behavioural correlations.
  • Performed feature engineering with Pandas and NumPy to prepare dataset for models.
  • Built deep learning models (TensorFlow + Keras) to map brain group activity to responses.
  • Recorded relations across neurons in different brain groups and their responses.
