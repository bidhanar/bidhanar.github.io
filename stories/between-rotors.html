Between Rotors

Short story — 2024

Between rotors the world becomes a compass of small decisions. The instruments hum in numbers but the mind wants to tell stories.

When the sun drops below the ridgeline, the control loop slows and memory sharpens its edges—solder and tea and the smell of damp tape. — (expand here)
